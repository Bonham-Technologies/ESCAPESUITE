ESCAPECRAFT can float its recorder controls. In Chrome and Edge, "Float controls" (next to the keyboard shortcuts under the record button) opens a small always-on-top window with the timer, the microphone and system-audio level meters, and Pause/Resume, Stop and Cancel, so you can record another app full screen and still see and stop the take without switching back. R, P and S work in that window too, and closing it leaves the recording running. It needs no network, so it works in the offline file as well.
