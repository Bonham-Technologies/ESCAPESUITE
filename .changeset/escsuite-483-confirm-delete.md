Deleting a recording in ESCAPECRAFT now asks first. The question names the recording and, when a take was recorded as several files (a separate webcam, microphone or system audio track), says how many files will be deleted. Cancel or Escape keeps it, and only the dialog's Delete button removes it. The question appears inside ESCAPECRAFT itself, so it also works when ESCAPECRAFT is embedded in another page.
