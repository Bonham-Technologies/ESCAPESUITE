The hosted site now works offline after the first visit. Once you have opened the hub, ESCAPECRAFT or ESCAPEARTIST on www.escapesuite.io while connected, that app opens and works with no connection, recording, editing and exporting included; open each app you want once while online. Chrome and Edge also offer to install each app. When a new version is published, the app saves it the next time you open it online and shows a short "Updated" note. Your recordings and projects are stored as before; this saves only the apps themselves.
