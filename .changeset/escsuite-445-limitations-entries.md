The Known limitations page now covers two more browser limits: Transcribe to captions in ESCAPEARTIST (it works in browsers with on-device speech recognition, today Chrome-family browsers on a computer, in real time, with a speech language pack your browser may download and no audio leaving your device) and Float controls in ESCAPECRAFT (only in browsers with Document Picture-in-Picture, such as Chrome and Edge, and not when embedded in another page).
