ESCAPEARTIST can now make captions from your timeline's sound with "Transcribe to captions", in browsers that can recognise speech on your device (Chrome on desktop from version 139). The speech recognition runs in your browser on this device, and your audio never leaves it. If your browser needs a speech language pack first, ESCAPEARTIST tells you before your browser downloads it from its maker (Google, for Chrome). The captions land on the Captions track as ordinary caption clips you can edit, and one undo removes them all. Browsers that can't recognise speech on your device don't show the button.
