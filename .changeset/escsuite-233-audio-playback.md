A microphone-only recording plays with a speaker glyph instead of a black box, the player's error message says "recording", and the time and the name in its controls no longer run together.

Playing back a take recorded with only the microphone used to open a black 300 by 150 box, and the player's failure message called every recording a "video". The player now shows a speaker icon on its dark surface for a take with no picture (clicking it still plays and pauses), the message reads "This recording could not be played.", and the time readout and the recording's name are spaced apart.
