Changing a clip's speed moves the clips after it on its own track, but captions, overlays and markers on other tracks stay where they were. ESCAPEARTIST now says so in a notice, in the same words Remove silences uses (for example "1 clip on other tracks did not move: 1 caption. 1 marker did not move."), so you know what may now be out of step. Nothing is shown when nothing was left behind, and screen readers announce the notice.
