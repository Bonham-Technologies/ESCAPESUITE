Labels and icons on blue buttons are now dark instead of white, so they are easier to read in both themes: the play button, the pressed B and I buttons and the other pressed buttons in the inspector, their small badges, the media library's Add button and audio badge, and the button that opens the inspector on small screens. The Add Track and transcription buttons keep their lettering readable when you point at them. In the light theme, the outline around the selected clip on the timeline, around a pressed button and around the drop area while you drag a file over it is a darker blue, so it is easier to see.
