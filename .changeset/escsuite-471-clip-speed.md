ESCAPEARTIST can speed a clip up or slow it down, from 0.25× to 4×, so the waiting parts of a screen recording play faster. Select a video or audio clip and open Speed in the inspector: choose 0.25×, 0.5×, 1×, 1.5×, 2× or 4×, or type any speed in between. The clip gets shorter or longer on the timeline, the clips after it on its row move up or along to match, and a recording's linked parts (its microphone, say) change speed with it so they stay in step. The sound plays faster or slower with the picture, so its pitch goes up or down, up to 2×; faster than 2× the clip is silent, and the inspector says which. To speed up just one part, split the clip around it first. Speed shows in the preview and in MP4, WebM and GIF exports, is kept when you save the project, and is rendered the same way by the headless render kit. Projects saved before open unchanged.
