The ESCAPE Suite home page and its legal pages have a new look, shared with the other Bonham Technologies products: warm paper and ink colours in light and dark, the Archivo typeface, square corners, and a teal accent. "Start recording" is now the one highlighted button; the other buttons are outlined. Nothing about what the pages say or where the buttons go has changed, and ESCAPECRAFT and ESCAPEARTIST look as they did.
