A recording that cannot start now says which source failed. When the camera is refused after you have already chosen what to share, ESCAPECRAFT used to say "The browser refused the capture", which read as if the screen share had failed; a camera that was missing or in use by another app got only "The recording could not be started." The notice now names the camera, the screen or the microphone, and says whether permission was refused or the device was not found or in use — for example, "The camera could not be opened (not found, or in use by another app) — nothing was recorded."
