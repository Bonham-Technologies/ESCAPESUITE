ESCAPECRAFT now keeps each recording on your device while it records. If the tab closes or crashes, or the recording ends in an error before it is saved, ESCAPECRAFT offers it back the next time you open it: Recover saves what was recorded to your library, and Discard deletes it. A recovered recording can be missing its last few seconds. In a browser that cannot keep the recording on the device (including Safari before 18.4 for screen-with-webcam-overlay and sound-only recordings, which it records as MP4), ESCAPECRAFT records in memory as before and tells you the recording is not protected if the tab closes. The Known limitations page now explains what this needs and what it can lose.
