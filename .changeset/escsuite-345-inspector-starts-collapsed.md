On a phone or a narrow window (below 900 pixels wide), ESCAPEARTIST now opens with the inspector panel tucked away instead of covering the preview, the media library and most of the timeline. Tap the round inspector button to slide it out. Selecting a clip doesn't open it for you. Once you open or close the inspector yourself, resizing the window leaves it the way you set it until you reload. Wider windows open with the inspector showing, as before.
