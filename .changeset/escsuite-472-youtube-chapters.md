The export dialog has a new Chapters row. It copies your timeline markers as a YouTube chapter list ("0:00 Intro", "0:15 Setup" and so on), or saves them as a .txt file. Times count from the start of the section you exported, markers outside it are left out, and an unlabelled marker is named "Chapter 2" and so on. If YouTube would not accept the list (fewer than three chapters, or one shorter than ten seconds), it tells you which rule, and still lets you copy it.
