The export dialog has a new Chapters row. It copies your timeline markers as a YouTube chapter list ("0:00 Intro", "0:15 Setup" and so on), or saves them as a .txt file. Times count from the start of the video you export: with in and out points set, you choose the section (markers outside it are left out) or the full video, and a marker with no label is named by its place, such as "Chapter 2". If YouTube would not accept the list (fewer than three chapters, or one shorter than ten seconds), it tells you which rule, and still lets you copy it.
