Every control in the clip inspector now has a name a screen reader can read. The panel's sliders, dropdowns, colour swatches, the font-size field and the text area were laid out beside a label that was never wired to them, so a screen reader announced most of the inspector as "slider, 50%"; each one is now named by the word next to it — or, where there is no visible word, by what a sighted user would call it — and the delete button, the aspect-ratio padlock and the no-fill toggle are named by something better than an icon's tooltip.
