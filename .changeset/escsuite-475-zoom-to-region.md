New: Zoom to region. Select a video clip, put the playhead where the zoom should start and choose "Zoom to region" in the clip's Actions. Drag a box over the part of the preview you want to fill the frame, or type its position and size, then pick how long to hold it (3 seconds to start with), how long the zoom in and out take (half a second) and the easing. Apply eases in to the box, holds it and eases back out. The zoom is made of ordinary keyframes you can see and change in the keyframe panel, and one undo removes it. The box keeps the video's shape and stays on the picture, so no black edges show, and if the clip already animates its position or scale during that time you are told which keyframe is in the way instead.
