After your first saved recording, import or project save in a visit, ESCAPECRAFT and ESCAPEARTIST ask your browser to keep their stored recordings and media instead of clearing them automatically, and say what it answered: "Protected from automatic clean-up", or "Your browser may delete these if it runs low on space; download what you want to keep." ESCAPECRAFT shows this under your recordings, ESCAPEARTIST beside the storage figure in the media library. The Known limitations page now explains that Safari can delete stored recordings and projects after seven days of using Safari without interacting with the site.
