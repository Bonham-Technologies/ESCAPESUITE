Two clips of one recording on screen at once now each show their own moment

A clip split and crossfaded into itself, or the same recording used twice at once — a zoomed copy on a track above, a picture-in-picture of itself — now shows each clip's own frame in the preview and in every export. Before, both clips showed whichever moment was asked for last: the crossfade faded between two copies of the incoming frame, and the zoomed copy and the full picture showed the same instant. A recording only takes a second player while two of its clips actually overlap; everything else costs what it did.
