Screen-and-webcam recordings made in Chrome or Edge now show their true colours when you open them in Firefox, in ESCAPEARTIST's preview and in what it exports there. They used to carry an invisible transparency layer, and Firefox draws any video with one in shifted colours (reds and greens off by up to about a seventh). Recordings made before this update keep the layer and still look shifted in Firefox; in Chrome and Edge they look right.
