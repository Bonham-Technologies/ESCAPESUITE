A project carrying overlays from an older ARTIST version now brings them onto the timeline as ordinary overlay clips when it loads, so they can be selected, restyled, moved, trimmed and deleted — and, for the first time, they are included in exports and headless renders instead of being silently dropped. One visible change comes with that: a blur region from an older version, which used to draw nothing at all, now blurs the video underneath it.
