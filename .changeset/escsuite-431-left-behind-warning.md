The Remove silences dialog now warns you when the cut will leave something where it is while the rest closes up: it says how many clips on other tracks will not move (blurs, captions, text or shapes, media clips) and how many markers, so you know what may fall out of step. With "Move everything after each cut" on, only what sits on a locked track is counted. The message after the cut repeats the count, and nothing is shown when everything moves.
