ESCAPECRAFT and ESCAPEARTIST now show which version you are running. In ESCAPECRAFT it is at the foot of Recording Tips (the Help button); in ESCAPEARTIST it is at the foot of the keyboard shortcuts sheet (press `?`). The offline download also says "offline file", so you can tell an old downloaded copy from the hosted app. Include that line when you report a problem.
