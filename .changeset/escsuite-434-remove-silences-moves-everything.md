Remove silences can now move everything after each cut, so blurs, text, shapes, captions, markers and in/out points stay on the moment they were placed on instead of drifting out of step. The new "Move everything after each cut" box in the Remove silences dialog is on by default. Something that spans a removed pause gets shorter rather than being cut in two, a locked track stays where it is, and one undo puts it all back. Clear the box to cut only the take's own tracks, as before.
