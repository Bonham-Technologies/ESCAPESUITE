Crop a clip from the inspector: four insets and aspect presets

A video or image clip can now show just part of its source. The new Crop section in the clip inspector gives you a percentage off each edge — drag the slider or type the number — plus one-click 1:1, 16:9, 9:16 and 4:3 presets and a Reset. Cropping shrinks the picture in place rather than stretching what is left over the old frame, so the clip stays where you put it and keeps its proportions. The preview, both export formats and server-side renders all honour the crop, and so do the selection box and the handles round the clip, so what you grab is what you see. A crop travels with the clip through a split, a duplicate, a copy and paste, an undo and a saved project file. On-canvas crop handles are still to come.
