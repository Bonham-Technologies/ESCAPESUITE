Your screen now stays on while ESCAPECRAFT records and while ESCAPEARTIST exports or transcribes, so a long job is not cut short by the display going to sleep. The export window says "Keeping the screen on until the export finishes." while it holds the lock. Browsers release the lock when the tab is hidden, and the apps take it again when you come back to the tab. Browsers without this feature work as before. The Known limitations page now explains that recording another window with ESCAPECRAFT hidden lets the screen sleep as your system settings say.
