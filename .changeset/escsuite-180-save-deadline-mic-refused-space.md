A stuck thumbnail no longer freezes a save, a refused microphone no longer costs you the window you just shared, and Space presses the button you have focused

Three fixes to ESCAPECRAFT. **The save can no longer get stuck.** Saving a recording decodes one frame of it for the library tile, and a file the browser's decoder would neither open nor reject left the app sitting on "Saving…" with no way out but reloading the tab. That decode now gives up after a few seconds and the recording is saved with a plain placeholder tile instead — the same tile a recording whose frame cannot be read has always got. **A microphone that cannot be opened no longer throws away your screen share.** If you picked a window and then the microphone prompt was refused, the device was already in use, or it was unplugged, the whole take was cancelled and you had to start again. Now the take goes ahead without the microphone, and the header says "The microphone could not be opened — recording without it." so you know the recording has no microphone in it. A refused screen share still cancels the take, because there would be nothing to record, and so does refusing the camera when you asked for a picture-in-picture overlay. **Space works on the playback dialog's Close button.** Pressing Space on it played or paused the video instead of closing the dialog; Space now presses whatever control you have focused, and is still play/pause when focus is on the video or the dialog itself.
