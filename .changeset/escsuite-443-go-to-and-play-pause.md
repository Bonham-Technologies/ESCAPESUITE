Each pause in the Remove silences list now has Go to and Play buttons, so you can check a pause before you cut it. Go to moves the playhead to the start of the pause. Play plays from one second before the pause to one second after it, then stops. Both work from the keyboard, and screen readers announce the pause's time with them. The row you last went to or played is highlighted and scrolled into view. On a narrow screen the buttons wrap below the times.
