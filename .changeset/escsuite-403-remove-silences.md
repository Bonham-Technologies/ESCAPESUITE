New in ESCAPEARTIST: **Remove silences**. Select a clip with sound and choose **Find silences** in the inspector's Actions to list every pause in the recording, shown on the timeline too. Adjust the threshold, the shortest pause to cut and the padding kept around each cut, keep any pause you want, then **Remove all** cuts them in one step that a single Ctrl+Z undoes. A separate-tracks recording from ESCAPECRAFT stays in sync: the screen, camera and microphone are cut together. It all runs in your browser, offline too.
